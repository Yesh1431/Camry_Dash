    How to run the catalyst test
    1. Park outdoors with the parking brake on, or have a passenger run the page while you drive. Let the engine warm up until Warm turns green.
    2. Hold the engine near 2,500 RPM for about 30 seconds, or cruise steadily at 45 to 60 mph. Watch both charts.
    3. Healthy cat: the lower chart stays fairly flat while the upper one swings. Worn cat: the lower chart swings like the upper one.
    4. Tap Reset low / high, snap the throttle open once and let go. A good downstream sensor reaches above 0.80 V, then drops below 0.20 V.
    5. Tap Copy last 10 minutes to keep the data.
