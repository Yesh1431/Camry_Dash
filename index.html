How to run the test

      
    
        
  1. Park outdoors with the parking brake on. Let the engine warm up until the Warm chip turns green.
    2. 
        
  2. Hold the engine near 2,500 RPM for about 30 seconds. Watch both charts.
    3. 
        
  3. Healthy cat: the lower chart stays fairly flat while the upper one swings. Worn cat: the lower chart swings like the upper one.
    4. 
        
  4. Tap Reset low / high, snap the throttle open once and let go. A good downstream sensor reaches above 0.80 V, then drops below 0.20 V.
